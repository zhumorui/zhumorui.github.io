Official code for [*Mind the Hitch: Dynamic Calibration and Articulated Perception for Autonomous Trucks*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23711) (CVPR 2026).

dCAP continuously estimates the 6-DoF relative pose between tractor and trailer cameras with a cross-view, temporal-attention transformer, and ships with the CARLA-based **STT4AT** benchmark for articulated semi-trailer trucks.
