LANTERN is a closed-loop benchmark for evaluating how vision-language models respond to safety warnings during cooperative driving. It separates warning onset, hazard onset, warning termination, and post-hazard recovery across diverse scenarios, using paired test conditions to isolate the impact of warnings on driving performance.
